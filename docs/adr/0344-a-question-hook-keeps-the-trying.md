# ADR 0344: A question hook keeps the trying

## Status

Accepted, Nov 25 2026 — Kasra's dictated rule, from his own
lines: "I'm trying to speak this thought after lunch before
it slips away" rates 7.5; the same line as a question — "Can
I say this thought aloud after lunch before it slips away?"
— rates 6.5, because the question drops the effort. His
order: make that fix for hook-line generation when the
question shape is picked.

## Context

The steering block's question line asked for one finished
question naming the feeling, goal-first, answer withheld.
It said nothing about the effort, so a picked question could
come back as a bare "can I" ask — a question about the act,
with the trying and its cost left out.

## Decision

The steering block's question line now carries Kasra's rule
in the generation itself: the question keeps the trying and
what it costs her; she asks from inside the struggle,
mid-attempt; never a bare "can I" ask that drops the effort
and asks about the act instead. The statement line is
untouched, and with no shape picked the prompt is unchanged.
This is a Kasra-dictated generation instruction, in the
class of the ADR 0316 / 0321 / 0340 exceptions to his
stop-tuning settlement.

## Consequences

A question pick steers toward the form Kasra rated 8 — the
question form of a trying line — instead of the 6.5 bare
ask. The hard gates do not change; the shape's finish and
budget rules stand as they were.
