# ADR 0280: The sub-headline names the video, captions and hashtags

Status: accepted

## Context

ADR 0276 set the hero sub-headline to Kasra's line: "No filming, no
editing, no blank page—get the hook, script, and finished cut
automatically." On Nov 23 2026 Kasra dictated a replacement that
names what the founder actually receives.

## Decision

The sub-headline now reads, verbatim: "No filming, no editing, no
blank page—get tailored video, captions and hashtags automatically."
The headline and the badge above it are unchanged. The render-smoke
pin that asserts the sub-headline moves in the same commit, with a
comment recording the change; no other copy, check, or file moves.

## Consequences

The hero promises the three deliverables by name — the tailored
video, its captions, and its hashtags — instead of the hook, script
and finished cut. ADR 0276's line stands in the record as the wording
it replaced after one day live.
