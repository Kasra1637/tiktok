# ADR 0316: Open on the moment

Status: accepted

## Context

The founder, after working a hook of his own aloud —
"I'm trying to say it Saturday morning before it races
off" — gave one instruction for the hook generator, in his
words: "Open on the moment, one concrete detail, the
resolution withheld; cut any app-behavior cue." (Nov 24
2026.) The shared spoken-line rules already carry pieces of
this — the goal-first opening, the withheld resolution in
HOOK POWER, the ban on app feature words — but nowhere does
one line state the hook's opening shape whole, and nothing
names the app-behavior cue (a hook that describes what the
app does or how it behaves) as a thing to cut.

## Decision

The instruction joins the shared spoken-line rules as its
own bullet, OPEN ON THE MOMENT, in the founder's words, for
the hook line only: open on the moment, one concrete
detail, the resolution withheld; cut any app-behavior cue.
It stands ahead of OPEN WITH THE GOAL, so the opening shape
is stated before the goal rule fills it in. Every surface
that writes a hook from the shared block — the plan, the
strategy, the hook rewrites — carries it at once. (Numbered
0316: 0315 is reserved for the wizard-rail build ordered
earlier the same night.)

## Consequences

The hook's first move is now one stated shape everywhere:
the moment, a single concrete detail, nothing resolved, and
nothing about the app's behavior. The block grows by one
short bullet; redundant phrasing elsewhere in the block was
trimmed to pay for it, and the strategy prompt's hard
ceiling still binds, verified by its budget test.
