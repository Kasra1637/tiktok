# ADR 0276: The sub-headline is Kasra's shorter line

Status: accepted

## Context

The hero sub-headline, kept from the parallel agent's copy pass by
Kasra's ADR 0268 ruling, read: "No filming, no editing, no blank
page. Turn your product facts into a hook, script, and finished
TikTok video — and get sharper with every clip you post." On Nov 22
2026 Kasra dictated a shorter replacement of his own.

## Decision

The sub-headline now reads, verbatim: "No filming, no editing, no
blank page—get the hook, script, and finished cut automatically."
The headline above it is unchanged. The render-smoke pin that asserts
the sub-headline moves in the same commit, with a comment recording
the change; no other copy, check, or file moves.

## Consequences

The hero says less and promises the outcome directly: the hook, the
script, and the finished cut arrive automatically. The ADR 0268 pins
otherwise stand — the headline and the rest of the kept copy are
untouched.
