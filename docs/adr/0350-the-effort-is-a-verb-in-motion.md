# ADR 0350: The effort is a verb in motion

## Status

Accepted, Nov 25 2026 — Kasra's dictated instruction, from
his own hook work the same evening. Rating "I'm trying to
name this tight feeling before lunch" at 7.5, he reached
for the stronger form himself — "I'm racing to name this
tight feeling before it slips" — liked it, and ordered
the pattern into hookline generation for any example and
any scenario.

## Context

The hook rules already required the cost to ride along
and the line to end mid-race (ADR 0321), but every
example the generator is shown opens with "I'm trying
to…", so that is the frame it reaches for — and "trying"
only states that she means to. The racing form puts her
mid-effort from the first word, against the loss the line
names, which is what the mid-race ending was always
meant to feel like from the start.

## Decision

HOOK POWER gains THE EFFORT IS A VERB IN MOTION: open on
the striving itself, already moving, never a bare
statement of intent; a verb of active effort (racing,
catching, holding on, chasing) carries the attempt, with
the loss riding in the same line. The verb is picked for
the scenario and changes with it, so the rule cannot
harden into every hook opening the same way. "I'm trying"
is not banned — settled 8/10 lines use it with the cost
riding — the generator simply reaches for the moving
verb first. A Kasra-dictated generation instruction, in
the class of the ADR 0316 / 0321 / 0340 / 0344 / 0346 /
0348 exceptions to his stop-tuning settlement.

Extended the same evening to the cover text, on Kasra's
order ("let's do that on the cover text too"): the cover
carries the race in miniature — THE RACE IN MINIATURE in
`_cover_text_rules_block` compresses a verb in motion
and what she stands to lose into the cover's 3 to 5
words, never a plain question and never a bare topic
label. The reasoning is his setup: the hook starts the
action, the Demo + CTA shows her in the middle of it, so
the cover — half of the first two seconds — hints at the
race instead of naming the topic.

## Consequences

Both passages fit the strategy prompt's standing
ceiling (50,200) without a raise — the fully populated
prompt stays under it, ceiling test green. Pinned by
backend tests on both blocks, beside the other
dictated-rule pins.
