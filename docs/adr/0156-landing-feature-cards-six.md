# ADR 0156: The landing feature grid — six cards, not eight

Status: accepted

Supersedes the eight-card "Everything between idea and posted" grid
and its shared 35-character title rule; the retired titles stay as
history in the commits that shipped them.

## Context

Kasra, on a voice call on Nov 15 2026, asked whether all eight
feature cards in the landing section "Everything between idea and
posted" are necessary, or whether some can be eliminated. The
answer he accepted: two of the eight are not separate steps of the
journey the section promises. The templates card (the opening
ranked to earn the watch) and the craft-grade card (a grade before
you pay) are beats inside the plan step — the same moment the
script card already describes — not steps of their own.

He also asked for shorter card titles: first a 20–23 character
draft set for the eight, then, once the cut to six was decided,
each title exactly one word shorter than those drafts.

## Decision

The grid carries exactly SIX cards, one per step of the journey:

1. "Facts you approve" — the App Profile.
2. "One clear outcome" — the angle and goal.
3. "Your voice, one script" — the plan: hook and script.
4. "Never filmed" — the render from one photo.
5. "Demo, finished" — Demo + CTA.
6. "Results that teach" — posted results steering the next video.

The templates and craft-grade cards are removed as standalone
cards. Their substance folds into card 3, whose new description
carries all three beats: the hook and script written in the user's
own voice from their facts, the opening ranked to earn the watch,
and the craft grade shown before they pay. Nothing true is lost;
the beats simply stand where they happen, inside the plan.

Every other surviving card keeps its description byte-identical.
All six descriptions remain exactly 160 characters — the uniform
length that keeps the grid free of ragged empty space — and the
smoke suite now pins the six exact titles and the uniform 160
instead of the retired shared 35-character title rule. Titles no
longer share one length; short and even is the rule now.

The grid drops its four-column xl step, so six cards lay out three
by two at desktop widths and two by three below — no ragged
second row. Card design, icons and section copy are otherwise
untouched; each surviving card keeps its icon, and card 3 keeps
the script card's.
