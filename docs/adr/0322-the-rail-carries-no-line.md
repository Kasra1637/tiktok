# ADR 0322: The rail carries no line

Status: accepted

## Context

Hours after ADR 0315 shipped the rail's gradient progress
fill and half-length active underline, the founder looked
at the rail again and reversed himself (Nov 24 2026):
"remove the line inside the rail" — and, asked which
line, answered with the design instead: "Only show the
check icon while the user go through the progress."

## Decision

The Create Video wizard rail carries no line at all. The
progress fill under the segments is removed, and so is
the active segment's underline (both ADR 0315, superseded
before either reached his phone a second time). Progress
reads from the check badges alone: a finished step wears
its filled check badge — pulse and all (ADR 0283) — the
rest keep their number or lock, and the active step is
marked by its indigo label alone. The percentages stay:
the founder ruled the same morning that he likes them
shown, and his reversal named the line, not the numbers.
The rail keeps no border, no card and no resting fill
(ADR 0298), and the phone shape is untouched (ADR 0319).

## Consequences

The rail is now badges, words and numbers — the quietest
it has ever been, with progress told by the one signal
that needs no legend. ADR 0315 stands in the record as
the design it briefly was; this record is why it left.
