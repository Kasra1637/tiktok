# ADR 0311: The shift lands before the last line

Status: accepted

## Context

The founder pasted the Craft check's story-arc readout for a
live pair (Nov 23 2026): hook and script work as one story —
problem, deepening, an ending that opens the demo; insight, a
mid-story shift and answering the hook early earn; the open
ending is paid in Demo handoff; repeating the hook, quoting
or wrapping up loses. On that clip the script echoed the
hook's image and kept its concrete action before the insight,
but it did not answer the hook in its first clause, and its
close drifted instead of opening the demo. His order: make
this improvement to the next model being added — it rides
with the Muse Ember restoration (ADR 0310).

## Decision

The improvement goes into the shared spoken-line rules (the
one block every script draft is written under), in its
EXTENSION rule. Answering the hook in the first clause was
already there and stays. What joins it is the closing shape:
land the shift in feeling BEFORE the last line — the last
line is not the payoff — and the last line stays open, on
the question the demo answers: a complete sentence whose
thought hangs toward the demo, never a wrap-up, a verdict or
the resolution; the demo completes it. The Craft scorer is
untouched — this changes what generation aims at, not the
checklist that reads it.

## Consequences

Every script surface that carries the shared block (plan,
strategy, remakes and rewrites through it) now aims at the
same arc the story-arc dimension scores: early answer,
mid-story shift, open final line. The closing shape is
folded into the arc sentence itself, and a few redundant
phrases elsewhere in the block were trimmed to pay for it —
the strategy prompt's hard ceiling (a truncated strategy is
a 502 the founder cannot fix) still binds, verified by its
budget test.
