# ADR 0303: The remake aims inside its band and gets a third attempt

Status: accepted

## Context

Sonnet 5.5's first live script remake (Nov 23 2026, the evening
it replaced Sol, ADR 0299) failed in the founder's hands: the
remake ran past the 180 characters the script's seconds hold on
both of the loop's two attempts — the correction naming the
overage each time — and the page raised "The remake kept
running long… try again, or edit the script yourself." The
founder's ruling was plain: the first generation should follow
the length rule; a user should never have to regenerate just
because a draft ran long. The remake's instruction stated the
band's two edges — the ceiling and the floor — but gave the
model nothing to aim at between them, and a draft aimed at an
edge lands past it as often as inside.

## Decision

rewrite_whole_script states an aim inside the band: with a
floor and a ceiling, the middle of the band (160 for the
140–180 shape the founder hit); with a ceiling alone, ten
characters under it. And the loop runs three attempts, the
count the plan chain and now the hook loop (ADR 0301) already
use. Nothing else moves: the band itself (ADR 0255), the named
corrections carrying the exact counts, the finished-sentence
gate, and the refuse-rather-than-store ending all stand. No
trimming a long draft down — a cut script is a broken script,
and the refusal stays the honest end when three drafts miss.

## Consequences

The first remake now aims at the middle of its room instead of
its walls, and a near-miss costs one more small call instead of
the founder's click. Three misses still refuse, with the last
miss named. The sibling single-line rewrites keep their own
loops; if one of them fails the same way in the founder's
hands, it gets the same treatment on its own evidence.
