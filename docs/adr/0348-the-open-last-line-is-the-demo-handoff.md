# ADR 0348: The open last line is the Demo handoff

## Status

Accepted, Nov 25 2026 — Kasra's dictated instruction, given
with its goal stated: enhance the Story arc / payoff score
on the first run. His words: answer the hook in the first
clause and land the shift before the last line; leave the
last line open, on the question the demo answers — that
ending is scored in Demo handoff. Never a quote, a
narration, a repeat of the hook, or a vague stand-in.

## Context

The arc itself already stood in the shared spoken-line
block (ADR 0311's EXTENSION rule): the answer in the first
clause, two beats max, the shift landed before the last
line, the last line open. What it did not say is what the
openness is FOR in the craft check's own terms — Demo
handoff scores that ending — nor did it carry Kasra's four
bans as one named list for the script as a whole. A script
rated the same evening answered loosely, named the act in
its first clause, and repeated a word: mid scores on the
first run, repaired by hand.

## Decision

The EXTENSION rule now closes with Kasra's sharpening: the
open last line is what Demo handoff scores — it hands the
viewer to the demo, or the score falls — and the script as
a whole is never a quote, never a narration, never a
repeat of the hook, and never a vague stand-in. A
Kasra-dictated generation instruction, in the class of the
ADR 0316 / 0321 / 0340 / 0344 / 0346 exceptions to his
stop-tuning settlement.

## Consequences

The strategy prompt's fully populated form measures
49,624 chars with this and ADR 0346's passage, so its
ceiling rises by a measured 700 to 50,200 (the ADR
0084/0090 practice), keeping ~575 of headroom. Pinned by a
backend test on the block, beside the other dictated-rule
pins.
