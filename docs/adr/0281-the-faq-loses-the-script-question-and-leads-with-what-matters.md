# ADR 0281: The FAQ loses the script question and leads with what matters

Status: accepted

## Context

ADR 0278 grew the landing FAQ to eight questions matched to the app
today. On Nov 23 2026 Kasra ordered one entry removed and the set
restructured so the most important questions stand at the top.

## Decision

The entry "Can I change the script before generating?" is removed
whole — question and answer. The remaining seven questions are
reordered most-important-first:

1. What do I need to get started?
2. How does my photo become a video that stops the scroll?
3. What is Hook + CTA?
4. Does the video use my real face and voice?
5. Does HookForge post or track results for me?
6. Can I use footage I already have?
7. Will my clip have sound, and how can I export it?

Every surviving answer stands word for word. The render-smoke
question-count pin moves from eight to seven in the same commit.

## Consequences

The FAQ opens with the two questions a founder decides on — what it
takes to start, and how a photo becomes a video that stops the
scroll — and ends with the technical details. Nothing else on the
landing page changes.
